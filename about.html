About IOT BETA

From curiosity to innovation.

IOT BETA is built around a simple idea: students can learn technology by actually building things.

Mission

Make robotics, IoT, electronics and STEM feel practical, understandable and exciting through projects and community learning.

Vision

Create a growing ecosystem where students can learn, build, share ideas, develop portfolios and explore technology careers.
